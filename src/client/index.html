How it works

  1. Send a POST to /api/rewrite with {"text": "your text"}
  2. Receive a 402 with payment requirements (x402 protocol)
  3. Sign the EIP-712 typed data with your wallet
  4. Resend with the PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header
  5. Receive {"rewritten": "..."}
